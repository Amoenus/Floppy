Description

Floppy is an open-source, self-hosted media tracker and personal diary. Track, rate, and organize movies, shows, video games, books, music, podcasts, anime, comics, and more in one unified library.

Floppy began as a fork of Yamtrack (opens in a new tab) and has diverged substantially since. Yamtrack CSV exports import directly.
